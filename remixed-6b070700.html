The practice runs on two kinds of capability: the teachable, technical hard skills the craft is built on, and the human and cognitive soft skills that align, question, and carry the work across teams.

Across every role, AI is becoming a partner in the work. It's fastest where it accelerates research, drafting, review, and maintenance, and most valuable when paired with the practitioner's judgment about what users actually need.
